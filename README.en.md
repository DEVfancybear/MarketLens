## Better decisions begin before the order

Traders often read the market in one place, validate an idea somewhere else,
execute in a separate terminal, then open yet another tool to record and review
the result. Every switch loses context, repeats work, and creates another chance
for the original plan to drift away from the final decision.

MarketLens brings that entire journey into one workspace. An idea can begin as a
drawing on the chart, be rehearsed through Replay, become a plan with entry,
stop loss, and take profit, pass through a risk check, and continue into alerts,
journaling, and performance analytics.

## From observation to experience

<div align="center">

**Observe** → **Build the scenario** → **Replay** → **Control risk** → **MT5** → **Stay alerted** → **Review**

</div>

MarketLens is shaped around a disciplined trading loop:

- **See the market your way** — multi-pane charts, indicators, precision drawing
  tools, templates, and workspaces preserve the context behind an idea.
- **Practice before capital is at risk** — Market Replay and simulated trading
  turn historical data into a training ground for both strategy and process.
- **Turn drawings into plans** — Long/Short Position tools prepare entry, stop
  loss, take profit, and position size directly from the chart.
- **Put risk before reward** — decisions are framed through position sizing,
  expected loss, and predefined limits before an order is sent.
- **Manage multiple MT5 accounts** — monitor and execute across accounts while
  retaining independent control over every trading destination.
- **Step away without losing awareness** — price and geometry alerts can reach
  the app, browser, Telegram, or Discord when a condition is triggered.
- **Turn outcomes into insight** — the journal and analytics track P/L, win
  rate, profit factor, drawdown, expectancy, and performance over time.

## The difference is continuity

| You want to | MarketLens helps you |
| --- | --- |
| Preserve an idea from analysis to execution | The visual plan continues through Replay, the order ticket, and the final review. |
| Test a method without fooling yourself | Replay, simulation, and journaling form a feedback loop you can repeat. |
| Trade several accounts without losing control | Every account and copy target keeps its own state and limits. |
| Leave the screen without missing an important condition | Alerts continue watching prices and the geometric zones you marked. |
| Keep the same process wherever you trade | The experience stays consistent across desktop and mobile, in English and Vietnamese. |

## For traders who value process

MarketLens is for discretionary traders who want visual analysis and disciplined
execution in the same flow; learners rehearsing a method through Replay; people
managing multiple MT5 accounts; and anyone who wants trade history to become
useful evidence for the next decision.

It is not a promise of profit. MarketLens is a toolkit for preparing more
clearly, controlling risk more deliberately, and learning more systematically
from your own decisions.
